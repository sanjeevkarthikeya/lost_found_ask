📖 How YenFind Works

A simple, secure, and privacy-preserving workflow designed specifically for Yenepoya University students and administration.

🎓 For Students & Finders

01
📝

Post Lost / Found

Fill in what you lost or found with campus zone and an optional reference photo. Our Smart Match Radar automatically alerts you if a matching item is already posted.

02
💬

Connect & Bystander Tips

Finders and owners connect privately to verify unique identifying details. Anyone who spotted the item on campus can drop a fast, anonymous Bystander Tip.

03
📜

Instant Verified Certificate

Upon meeting and handing over, tap Complete Handover. The app generates a 2x Retina Official Handover Receipt on your device with QR code & timestamps. 0 server cost!

🛡️ For Campus Administrators & Security

01
📁

Roster Import & Mode Toggle

Import student CSV rosters in seconds. Choose between Strict University Verification (validates college email) or Open Campus Mode (easy access).

02
📌

Broadcast Security Notices

Post pinned alerts for high-value items (laptops, wallets, keys) held at the Central Security Office with clear claim guidelines.

03
💾

CSV Backup & Semester Wipe

At the end of each semester, export full resolution archives with 1 click to CSV for official university records, then execute a clean semester reset.
