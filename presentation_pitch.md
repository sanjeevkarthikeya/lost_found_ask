# 🎤 YenFind - Hackathon Winning Pitch & Demo Script (3-Minute Presentation)

Use this step-by-step presentation script in front of the judges for **PromptWars x YenTech**!

---

### ⏱️ Minute 0:00 – 0:45 | The Problem & The Insight
> *"Respected judges, every semester across Yenepoya University's expansive campus—from the Central Library to the Canteen and Medical Blocks—hundreds of valuable items are lost: AirPods, student IDs, calculators, and wallets.
>
> Traditional lost-and-found WhatsApp groups or security desks have three critical flaws:
> 1. **Zero privacy** (personal phone numbers leaked).
> 2. **High server storage costs** when storing hundreds of resolved photos.
> 3. **No accountability or proof** of who actually handed the item to whom.
>
> We built **YenFind** — the smart, zero-server-cost, gamified lost-and-found recovery network for Yenepoya University."*

---

### ⏱️ Minute 0:45 – 1:45 | Live Demo Flow
1. **Live Feed & Flipkart-Style Filters:**
   * *"Notice our sleek glassmorphic interface. Students can instantly filter by category (Electronics, IDs, Stationery) and campus hotspots (Central Library, Canteen, Medical Sciences)."*
2. **Smart Match Radar (Innovation):**
   * *"When a student starts typing what they lost, our **Smart Match Radar** runs client-side keyword matching to check if someone already found a similar item—preventing duplicate clutter."*
3. **Person 3 Bystander Tips:**
   * *"If someone didn't pick up the item but saw it on desk 14, they can drop a 1-click **Bystander Tip** to guide the owner."*
4. **Client-Side Verified Handover Certificate (The Game Changer):**
   * *"When finder and owner meet, they tap **Complete Handover**. Instead of overloading the university database, our Canvas engine dynamically renders a **Retina-quality Official Digital Handover Certificate** with security QR codes and timestamps that auto-downloads directly to their devices. 
   * Resolved posts are **automatically pruned after 7 days**, keeping the platform 100% lightweight and fast."*

---

### ⏱️ Minute 1:45 – 2:30 | Gamification & Admin Powers
1. **Karma Hall of Fame (+Social Share Bonus):**
   * *"Students earn +100 Karma points for honest returns and +50 points when sharing their certificate to social media, fostering a culture of honesty on campus."*
2. **Admin Security Vault (PIN-Protected):**
   * *"The campus security team can upload student rosters via CSV, toggle between **Strict Roster Verification** and **Open Campus Mode**, broadcast pinned **Security Notices**, and perform a **1-Click Semester CSV Archival & Reset**."*

---

### ⏱️ Minute 2:30 – 3:00 | Cloud & Scalability
> *"YenFind is built with a modular database architecture ready for **Google Cloud Run** and Firebase Firestore deployment across all Yenepoya campuses. 
> 
> Thank you! We are ready for questions."*
